# 7habits — public legal & support pages

This public repo serves ONLY the pages at https://ndmr0.github.io/7habits/
(index.html, privacy.html, terms.html, support.html) via GitHub Pages. Those
exact URLs are referenced by the App Store listing and inside the shipped iOS
app — never rename this repo, move or rename these files, or break the URLs.
Change only the page text, and only when the legal/support content changes.

Everything else about 7habits lives in other repos: the product in
`7habits-app`, the 7habits.app site in `7habits-site`, and ad/social content
in `7habits-content`. Instructions that aren't about these pages belong there.
