You are setting up a new Facebook Page for **7habits**, an iOS habit-tracking

## What the app is

7habits tracks seven habits across a seven-day week: Prayer, Gratitude,
Connection, Exercise, Hydration, Sleep, and Nutrition — chosen to cover the
spiritual, emotional, and physical sides of a day. A streak counts consecutive
days you complete a habit. Every 7 days earns a small celebration; every 49 days
(seven weeks) completes a full cycle. Miss a day and the streak resets, but your
colour history stays. Data lives entirely on the device — nothing is uploaded,
no account is required, and you can export everything from Settings → Export
data.

## Rules you must not break

These are accuracy constraints, not style preferences. Breaking one creates a
false advertising claim.

1. **Do not write "free app", "free forever", or "free download".** The app is a
   paid subscription with a 30-day introductory trial. Whenever the trial is
   mentioned, it must be paired with the fact that it renews. The approved
   phrasing is: *"30 days free on Monthly or Yearly for new subscribers. It
   renews unless you cancel."*
2. **Do not put prices anywhere** — not in the About field, not in a post, not
   on an image. They vary by region and they change. Let the App Store show them.
3. **Do not soften or embellish the privacy claim.** The exact approved wording
   is *"No account. No cloud. Your data stays on your phone."* Do not upgrade it
   to "encrypted", "anonymous", "zero-knowledge", or "we never track you" —
   none of those have been verified and some imply things that aren't claimed.
4. **Do not describe the app as health, medical, mental-health, or wellness
   advice.** It tracks habits. It does not advise.
5. **Do not invent features.** No sync, no accounts, no reminders, no widgets,
   no Android version, no web app. If you're unsure whether something exists,
   leave it out.
6. **Do not use the Apple "Download on the App Store" badge** unless you take
   the official artwork from Apple's marketing guidelines. Do not redraw or
   approximate it.
7. **Use the copy below as written.** Fix a typo if you find one; don't rewrite
   for tone.
