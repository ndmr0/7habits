## Ten more video ideas

1. **The 49-day cycle.** Seven habits × seven days × seven weeks. Show the grid
   filling in.
2. **Why there's no account.** Screen-record opening the app and using it —
   no sign-up screen at all.
3. **What breaking a streak looks like.** The streak resets; the colour history
   doesn't. Miss a day, keep the record.
4. **Why seven and not thirty.** A week is a unit people can actually picture.
5. **Where the colours come from.** Each habit owns a hue; each day is a shade
   darker. The grid is the whole app in one image.
6. **Export your data.** Settings → Export data. Show the file. Nothing to
   delete from a server because nothing was ever on one.
7. **The habit you'd drop.** Ask which of the seven people would cut. Read the
   replies on camera later.
8. **Spiritual, emotional, physical.** Why the seven cover all three, not just
   the gym.
9. **A day in the app.** Thirty seconds, morning to night, seven taps.
10. **Reply to a comment with a video.** Cheapest reliable reach on TikTok.

## Caption patterns

Keep captions under two lines. The hook belongs on screen, not in the caption.

```
Habit 03 of 07: Connection. Reach out to someone today.
```

```
No account. No cloud. Nothing leaves your phone. That was the whole point.
```

```
Seven habits, seven days. Which one would you drop?
```

```
Day 4 of showing all seven. This one takes the least time and gets skipped most.
```

Ask a question in roughly one caption in three — comments are what the algorithm
reads as interest.

`#atomichabits` `#personalgrowth`

`#iosapp` `#appstore`

**By habit:** `#prayer` `#gratitude` `#connection` `#exercise` `#hydration`
`#sleep` `#nutrition`

`#buildinpublic` and `#indieapp` reach a smaller but far warmer audience than
the self-improvement tags. Worth one post a week.

## Claims: keep these honest

Two lines carry weight, and both appear on artwork:

**"No account. No cloud. Your data stays on your phone."**
True as the app stands: nothing is uploaded, no account is required, and data is
exportable from Settings. If sync, accounts, or analytics ever ship, this line
and every asset carrying it have to change.

**"Start free for 30 days."**
The 30-day introductory offer applies to Monthly and Yearly, only for Apple IDs
that haven't used it before. It converts to a paid subscription automatically
unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. Lifetime has no trial.

Say "30 days free, then it renews unless you cancel" rather than "free app" —
the second one reads as a lie the moment someone hits the paywall, and it's the
kind of thing that gets an App Store listing reported.

Current pricing, from `terms.html`: Monthly US$9.99 · Yearly US$59 · Lifetime
US$99 one-time. Don't put prices on artwork — they vary by region and they
change. Let the App Store show them.

## Posting

Three to five posts a week beats seven then nothing. The seven habit cards give
you two weeks at that rate before you have to make anything new.

Reply to every comment for the first month, in the app, from the account. That's
the single highest-leverage thing on a new TikTok account and it costs nothing.
